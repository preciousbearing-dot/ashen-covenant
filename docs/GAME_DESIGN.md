# Game Design — Ashen Covenant

## High concept

A battle mage survives the destruction of an ancient order and crosses a ruined kingdom to uncover why the dead are returning—and why the same force is awakening inside them.

**Genre:** Dark fantasy action RPG  
**Platform priority:** Android  
**Camera:** Angled top-down 3D  
**Structure:** Offline-first, single-player  
**Tone:** Ancient, ominous, tragic, epic; readable combat over visual noise

The title is a working title and must be checked before commercial release.

## Core loop

1. Explore a compact ruined location.
2. Read enemy intent and engage in deliberate real-time combat.
3. Defeat enemies and survive hazards.
4. Earn progress and reveal fragments of the central mystery.
5. Unlock the next encounter or route.

## Player fantasy

The player is a dangerous but vulnerable battle mage. Combat should feel precise and expressive rather than button-mashy. Positioning, timing, and spell choice should matter.

## Planned abilities

- **Arcane Bolt:** Fast, readable ranged projectile; first offensive ability.
- **Blink Step:** Short directional evade; first mobility ability.
- **Rift Burst:** Later area-of-effect spell.
- **Aegis Ward:** Later defensive barrier.

The first playable prototype should use only Arcane Bolt and Blink Step, one enemy archetype, health, and restart.

## Initial controls

- Left side: virtual movement stick.
- Right side: attack and dash buttons.
- HUD: health and a minimal resource indicator.
- All important actions must remain reachable on a phone screen.

## Visual direction

Use stylised, performance-conscious 3D: ash-black stone, muted iron, restrained gold accents, and focused blue-violet magic. Prioritise strong silhouettes, contrast, and combat readability over expensive realism.

## Scope guardrails

- No online multiplayer in the initial release.
- No open-world map in the prototype.
- No monetisation systems until the core game is fun and stable.
- Avoid expensive assets or paid services during the zero-budget proof of concept.
